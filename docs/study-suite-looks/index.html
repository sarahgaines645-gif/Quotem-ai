Study Suite — six looks

Same engine, six skins. Every card below is a working mock of the revision question card (four options, the WHY line, next) and THE TUBE (fills while you dither, flushes when you're right, splashes when you're wrong, overflows and ends the run). Answer a few — the animations are the point. Flip each one teen ↔ sensible. Pick one, or say “this card, that tube”.

Opens from disk, no network, no libraries. Sound is off unless you turn it on. Motion follows your device's reduce-motion setting; there's a switch too. Teen modes deliberately leave the app's neumorphic system; the sensible modes stay close to the paper-and-ink revise page.
